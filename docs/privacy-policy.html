Privacy Policy

Inkling keeps your writing on your iPhone. There is no account to create, nothing is sold, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers.

Your writing

Everything you write, and the days you mark as written, is stored on your device. The developer cannot read it. If you export your writing, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Reminders

The daily reminder is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Prompts and art

Every prompt and artwork ships inside the app, so Inkling works offline and requests nothing to show them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm them. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase records. It does not receive your writing. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

Clear the text of an entry and it is removed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Inkling is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
