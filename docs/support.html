Writing

Each day brings three prompts, each with a painting, photograph or print. Tap one and start writing under it. Your words save as you type, and the day gets its check.

Writing on paper

Open a prompt and tap Wrote It. The day is checked without typing anything in the app.

Your streak

Tap the week at the top of Today to see the calendar, your current streak, your longest streak and every day you wrote. A streak stays alive until the end of today, so there is still time.

Inkling Pro

Pro opens earlier days' prompts from the calendar and exports everything you have written. Manage or cancel in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Settings in Inkling and tap Restore Purchases.

The art

The artworks are in the public domain, from the collection of the Art Institute of Chicago.
