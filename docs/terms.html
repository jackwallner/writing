Subscription

Inkling Pro unlocks earlier days' prompts and exporting your writing, as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or as a single lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. A subscription renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period, and you can cancel any time in Settings, your name, Subscriptions. The lifetime purchase is paid once and does not renew.

Your writing

What you write is yours. The prompts and the public-domain artworks are offered for personal use.
