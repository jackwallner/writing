Each morning Inkling offers three writing prompts, each paired with a work of art: a painting, an old photograph, a Japanese woodblock print, a watercolor. Pick the one that catches you and write.

WRITE HERE OR ON PAPER
Write right under the prompt, with the picture above you. Your words save as you type. Prefer a notebook? Write on paper, tap Wrote It, and the day still counts.

A CALENDAR OF CHECKS
Every day you write gets a check. The week sits at the top of Today, and the calendar shows every month, your current streak, your longest streak and how many days you have written.

MADE FOR A DAILY HABIT
More than a year of prompts and art is built into the app, so it works offline, even on a plane. Set a quiet daily reminder for the time you like to write.

PRIVATE
No account. Your writing stays on your iPhone.

INKLING PRO
Today's prompts, writing, the calendar and your streak are free. Inkling Pro opens every earlier day's prompts from the calendar and exports everything you have written.
