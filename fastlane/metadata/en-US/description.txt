Each morning Inkling offers three writing prompts, each paired with a work of art: a painting, an old photograph, a Japanese woodblock print, a watercolor. Pick the one that catches you and write.

A GALLERY EVERY MORNING
Today opens like a small museum. Swipe between the day's three works, each shown whole, in a room lit by its own colors. Tap a painting to fill the screen and pinch in to look closely.

WRITE HERE OR ON PAPER
Write right under the prompt, with the picture above you. Your words save as you type. Set a timer for a five to thirty minute stretch. Prefer a notebook? Write on paper, tap Wrote It, and the day still counts.

A CALENDAR MADE OF PAINTINGS
Every day you write keeps the painting you wrote to. The calendar fills in month by month, beside your current streak, your longest streak, the days you have written and your word count.

GO DEEPER
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, ask for one question about what you just wrote. It runs on your iPhone and your words never leave it.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
A widget hangs the day's paintings on your Home Screen, a new one each morning, afternoon and evening, and puts the prompt on your Lock Screen.

SHARE A PAGE
Send a piece of writing as a card, set under its painting.

MADE FOR A DAILY HABIT
More than a year of prompts and art is built into the app, so it works offline, even on a plane. Set a quiet daily reminder for the time you like to write.

PRIVATE
No account. Your writing stays on your iPhone.

INKLING PRO
Today's prompts, writing, the calendar and your streak are free. Inkling Pro opens every earlier day's prompts from the calendar and turns everything you have written into a book, each piece under its painting.
